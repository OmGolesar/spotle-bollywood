★ QUESTIONS FROM THE AUDIENCE ★

FAQआपके सवाल

What is Spotle Bollywood?+

A daily puzzle where you guess one mystery Hindi film in ten tries. Each guess reveals eight comparison tiles and gradually un-blurs the poster.

When does the daily film reset?+

At midnight IST. Everyone playing on the same calendar day in India plays the same film.

What films can show up?+

You can guess any film in our 3,000+ Hindi film catalogue regardless of difficulty. Difficulty controls which film is picked as today's mystery — Easy picks modern blockbusters, Medium well-known hits across decades, Hard adds parallel, cult and deep-cut cinema.

Why isn't my film showing up in the search?+

The catalogue is 3,000+ films but not exhaustive — some older or less-reviewed films are missing. If a title you expected isn't appearing, drop me a line via the footer and I'll check whether it should be added.

Can I play yesterday's puzzle?+

Not yet — the archive is coming soon. For now each day's puzzle is one-shot.

How do hints work?+

You get two hints per play, unlocked after your third and sixth guess. Each hint reveals one of: tagline, filmography, or a cast member — you pick which.

What counts as a close match?+

Close (mustard) depends on the attribute. Year is within 5 years. Box office is within 50% of the answer's gross. IMDb is within 0.5 points — teal means both films display the exact same rating. Director and music turn mustard when the two people are frequent collaborators (same cluster across films).

What does the box-office figure represent?+

Worldwide gross from TMDB, converted to an approximate crore equivalent for the display. It's not the same as India-only collections — this metric is used so the game can score large and small releases on the same scale.

What are the three difficulty modes?+

Easy covers 500 modern blockbusters. Medium covers 1,500 well-known hits across decades. Hard adds parallel, cult and deep-cut cinema from the full catalogue — streaks there are the real brag.

Does the site track me?+

No login required for playing. A single cookie stores your streaks and today's progress. No analytics, no third-party trackers.

How to playकैसे खेलें

Guess today's mystery Hindi film in 10 tries. Each guess reveals 8 color-coded tiles comparing your pick to the answer — director, cast, year, box office, music, banner, genre, IMDb score.

  • ✓Teal — this attribute matches today's film exactly.
  • ~Mustard — partial match. Close, but not quite.
  • ✕Red — no overlap.

The poster un-blurs with every guess. Use up to 2 hints per play (unlocked after guess 3 and guess 6). Three difficulty modes refresh daily at midnight IST.
